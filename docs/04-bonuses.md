## Bonus 1 — The AI ICP Engine (Claude Skill File)

**Stated value: ₹5,000**

> You know that feeling when a training ends and you're left wondering, "okay, now who do
> I actually target?" This is exactly why we built this skill. Feed it your business
> context, and it builds **5–10 ready-to-scrape ICPs in minutes** — pain points, buying
> logic, and hard filters all worked out for you.

### What it does

| Input | Output |
| --- | --- |
| Your business context | 5–10 distinct ICPs, each with: pain points · buying logic · hard filters ready to scrape |

This is the **first of the two AI skill files** that power the system. It is the engine
behind Phase 2, and the thank-you page instructs buyers to run it *before touching copy* —
"copy written against a vague list is what kills most first campaigns."

### The stall it removes

The gap between "I understand the system" and "I know who to send to." Without it, the
buyer finishes the training and immediately has to guess at their own buyer definition —
which is where most first campaigns die.

### Creative note

The illustration is abstract on purpose (`design/bonus-icp-engine.html`): an unfiltered
market narrowing through a filter into a handful of defined customer profiles. A skill
file has no capturable UI worth screenshotting.

## Bonus 2 — The Outbound Copywriting Skill

**Stated value: ₹7,000**

> Good targeting still fails with bad copy, and copy is usually where people get stuck
> longest. This is the exact skill used to write every sequence in the live build,
> **trained on real emails that actually got replies**, not generic templates. Once your
> ICP is ready, this turns it into copy in minutes, in the same voice and structure that's
> already working across real campaigns.

### What it does

The **second AI skill file**. Takes a finished ICP and produces sequence copy built around
the system's governing rule: **never ask for a call first**. Ask for something small
instead — a free Loom, a quick pilot.

It is trained on real, high-converting sent emails rather than template libraries, so the
voice and structure match what is already working in live campaigns.

### The stall it removes

Copywriting is named on the page as the step people get stuck on *longest*. This turns a
multi-day blocker into minutes.

### Relationship to OB1

Bonus 2 **generates** copy. OB1 (the swipe file) is the **reference bank** to check
generated copy against. They are complementary, not overlapping — which is why OB1 still
converts as a paid bump despite Bonus 2 being free.

## The two Claude skill files

Bonuses 1 and 2 are the mechanical core of the whole offer, not add-ons. The sales page
describes them as *"2 AI skill files that do the heavy lifting"* — one builds the exact
target list (pain points, buying logic and all), the other writes the copy that gets
replies.

They are what allows the FAQ to answer "do I need any technical skills?" with a flat
**no**: the two skill files handle the heaviest technical lifting.

                                                          ▼
