## 4. The three phases

The three phases run in the same order every time, powered by **2 AI skill files** that
do the heavy lifting: one builds the target list, the other writes the copy.

### Phase 1 — BUILD: The Burn-Proof Deliverability System

*Infrastructure setup*

Most people burn their main inbox within days of sending cold email from it. This phase
makes sure that never happens.

- Dedicated domains, separate from the primary business domain
- US-IP mailboxes
- DNS (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) handled **automatically** rather than manually
- The exact domain-to-mailbox ratio that keeps infrastructure from being blacklisted
- A 14-day warm-up before a single email goes out
- Monthly cost maths worked out live

**End result:** infrastructure built to send **1,000+ emails a day** without ever
touching the primary domain's reputation.

> The 14-day warm-up starts the moment Phase 1 is finished, which is why the thank-you
> page tells buyers to build Phase 1 *first* rather than binge all three.

### Phase 2 — TARGET: The AI ICP Engine

*Targeting and lead sourcing*

Wrong targeting kills campaigns before copy even matters.

- Feed business context into a purpose-built AI skill (Claude)
- It generates **5–10 ICPs**, each with clear pain points, buying logic and hard filters,
  ready to scrape
- A second tool pulls and verifies the contact list against those filters
- This replaces the multi-tool stack — and the ~50% bad-email rate that comes with it
- The exact filtering process is shown live

**Governing principle:** 80% list accuracy plus the right sequence beats an expensive
4-tool stack every single time.

### Phase 3 — LAUNCH: The Low-Resistance Copy & Launch Blueprint

*Copy and launch*

Even the right list fails with the wrong ask.

- A second AI skill writes copy trained on real, high-converting emails
- Built around one rule: **never ask for a call first**. Ask for something small
  instead — a free Loom, a quick pilot
- Spintext variations, so one email becomes five
- Spam-check process before launch
- The real client campaign configured and launched live inside Instantly

**Result:** 2,000 leads reached · 90 replies · 19 qualified in 2 days.
