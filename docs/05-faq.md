# 05 - FAQ

The questions that come up every time.

---

### Will this work for my business?

If you're B2B or SaaS and your buyers are reachable at a work email address, yes - that's
exactly who it's built for. It works especially well targeting the US, Europe or India.

It is **not** built for local, offline businesses, or for anyone selling directly to
individual consumers.

### I've tried cold email before and it didn't work. Why would this be different?

Most failed attempts skip the infrastructure work entirely - sending from a single inbox,
no warm-up, no dedicated domains. That's usually what kills deliverability before the copy
or the targeting even gets a chance to matter.

This system fixes that first, which is why Phase 1 comes before anything else.

### Do I need technical skills?

No. Domain purchases, DNS setup, mailbox warm-up, ICP building and copywriting are all
walked through step by step, and the two AI skill files handle the heaviest technical
lifting.

If you can follow a sequence of steps, you can build this.

### How long until I see results?

The build itself is one sitting. **Infrastructure maturity is what takes time** - domains
need to age and mailboxes need 14 days of warm-up before you launch, so plan on roughly 30
days for the setup to fully mature.

That said, campaigns following this system have generated qualified replies within days of
going live. The live campaign built in the session returned 90 replies and 19 qualified
conversations in its first 2 days.

### What will the tools cost to run?

Domains, mailboxes and a sending platform are the real cost of running outbound. The exact
figure depends on your target send volume.

**Run the Cost + ROI Calculator before you buy anything** - it's the same one behind every
cost number shown in the session. Enter your mailbox count and lead volume and you'll see
your real monthly cost, and what the campaign needs to return to justify it.

### I'm just getting started and don't have clients yet. Is this still for me?

Yes, as long as you're selling to businesses and your buyers are reachable by email.

Starting from zero is arguably the *better* case - you build the infrastructure correctly
the first time, instead of undoing a burned domain and a bad sending history later.

### Can't I just send from my existing Gmail or Outlook?

You can, once. Then your primary domain's reputation is damaged, and that domain also
carries your invoices, your client threads and your password resets.

Domain reputation is not something you can meaningfully repair on a useful timeline. This
is the single most expensive mistake in outbound, and it's the reason Phase 1 exists.

### Why 40+ mailboxes instead of one inbox sending carefully?

Because low volume per mailbox is what keeps you safe, and total volume is what generates
replies. Those two only reconcile by spreading sends across many mailboxes.

Forty warmed mailboxes each sending a modest daily volume is far safer - and produces far
more - than one inbox pushing 50 emails a day.

### Why shouldn't I ask for a call in the first email?

Because you're asking a stranger for 30 minutes before you've given them anything. The ask
is too large for the relationship.

Ask for something that costs them one word to accept instead - a free Loom, a small pilot,
a resource. The call comes after they've said yes to something small.

### Do I need a huge tech stack?

No - and the belief that you do is the most expensive myth in the list. **80% list accuracy
and the right sequence beats an expensive multi-tool stack every time.**

Most over-tooling is people stacking four verification tools to fix one bad data source.
Fix the source instead.

### Should I run LinkedIn at the same time?

Not at the same time as setting up email. Get email live first, then add LinkedIn on the
same list.

The logic is good - you've already paid to find those contacts, and LinkedIn reaches the
same people at no extra sourcing cost. But two half-built channels perform worse than one
finished one.

### What if my campaign underperforms?

Diagnose top-down, because each layer depends on the one above it:

1. **Delivery rate low?** Infrastructure problem - go back to Phase 1
2. **Delivery fine, opens low?** Sending reputation or subject lines
3. **Opens fine, no replies?** Targeting or the ask - Phase 2, then Phase 3
4. **Replies but unqualified?** Your ICP filters are too loose - Phase 2

Change one variable at a time, and give a full sequence cycle before judging it. The
diagnostics section of the
[implementation guide](https://claude.ai/public/artifacts/d403a11f-86b5-42d3-b1d1-ba87f84b4e0f)
walks through this symptom by symptom.
